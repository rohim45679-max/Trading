Uses the symbol's Binance market data. If no quote asset is given, USDT is assumed.
Each timeframe keeps its own clock in the background for every coin — the 3 min column refreshes every 3 minutes, 4 hour every 4 hours, whether or not it's the tab you're looking at — aligned to the exchange's real candle boundaries, plus a short buffer so the candle has actually closed. Switching tabs just shows what's already been fetched. Columns mirror the Python report: time (IST), high, low, close, volume, trade count. Coins you add are remembered on this device (browser local storage) and re-fetched on every visit. Data via the public Binance API, fetched directly in your browser — no server involved.
